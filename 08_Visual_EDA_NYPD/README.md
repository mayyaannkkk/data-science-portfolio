# Visual EDA -- NYPD Motor Vehicle Collisions

**Course:** DSA 506 Visual Analytics and Communications
**Dataset:** NYPD Motor Vehicle Collisions (NYC Open Data)

## Overview
Exploratory data analysis with visual storytelling on NYC motor vehicle collision data, built in R with ggplot2 in a Google Colab R-kernel notebook.

## Visualizations
- Time-of-day collision heatmap (hour x day of week)
- Borough-level bar chart and density plots
- Injury vs fatality stacked breakdown
- Contributing factor frequency (top 15)
- Monthly trend line

## Storytelling Arc
1. Hook: most dangerous hour in NYC
2. Context: borough rates and factors
3. Conflict: disproportionate injury in specific districts
4. Resolution: temporal and spatial action points
